Stuck, found a bug, or lost a purchase? Email thomasvallance13@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and what happened. We usually reply within two working days.

Restoring purchases

Premium and the Starter pack are tied to your Apple ID. On a new phone or after reinstalling, open the game, tap the gear (top right) → Restore purchases.

Ads

Ads are always optional and always pay out. If an ad won't load, try again in a minute — the game never charges you for a failed ad. Premium removes ads and grants their rewards instantly.

Where's my progress?

Your save lives on your phone. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
