Hustle Inc — Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026 · Developer: Thomas Vallance

The short version

Hustle Inc has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your game save is stored only on your device. The developer does not collect, see or sell your personal data. The game shows optional ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect some data to show and measure those ads, as described below.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress (cash, shop name, machines, staff, settings) is saved locally on your iPhone. It is not sent to the developer. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Rewarded ads are optional: you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward. To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when iOS asks), approximate location derived from your IP address, information about ads you see and interact with, and crash and performance diagnostics. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalised using your advertising identifier and nothing changes in the game. In the UK and EU you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change this choice at any time.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy · How Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer never receives your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to these transactions.

Children

Hustle Inc is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data held by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy controls at myaccount.google.com. You can reset your advertising identifier or limit tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
